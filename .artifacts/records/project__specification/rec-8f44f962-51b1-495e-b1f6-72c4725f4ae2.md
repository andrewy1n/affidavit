## Requirements

R1: A manifest claim may declare `evidence_kind` as one of `unit`, `integration`, or `interaction`.

R2: `aff verdict` fails a claim declared `interaction` whose witness runs under the default unit pytest runner template.

R3: `aff verdict` passes a claim declared `interaction` whose witness runs under an interaction runner template that produces JUnit outcomes `run_tests` understands.

## Acceptance criteria

- Missing or invalid `evidence_kind` handling is defined and tested (reject or default—plan-phase picks reject-invalid).
- Interaction + default runner ⇒ non-zero verdict.
- Interaction + interaction template fixture ⇒ pass when other verdict rules hold.

## Constraints

- Builds on `run_tests` and runner argv templates.
- `second-runner` may add another language template later; this phase needs at least one interaction template.
- No store tokens under `affidavit/`.

## Non-goals

- Full browser stack productization beyond a template that fixtures can run.
- Auto-detecting kind from paths.

## Invariants

- Kind mismatch cannot yield pass.
- Outcomes still come from JUnit via `run_tests`.

## Assumptions

- One interaction template (tmux, CLI e2e, or similar argv) is enough for R3.
