## Rationale

Every audit step reruns frozen tests, so the runner adapter comes first or the audit inherits pytest's output format. The verdict is the gate answer, so it is made trustworthy before the audit reports on it: the environment is frozen, the result is signed where the agent cannot run it, and the oracle's author and timing are checked from git. Evidence kind and standalone claim authoring follow because they extend what a claim is. The audit then reports what witnesses miss, blind spots before the less general differential. A second runner and an external end-to-end run prove the product outside this repo and outside orchestra.

## Counter-argument

The audit is the visible part of the product and the spikes already work, so shipping `aff audit` right after the runner adapter would show value sooner and let real reports decide which verdict hardening matters. Claim authoring is also what makes affidavit usable without orchestra at all, and putting it eighth delays the first outside user.
