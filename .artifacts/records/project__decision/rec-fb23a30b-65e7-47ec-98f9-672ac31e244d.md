## Rationale

Declared probes keep the false-positive rate manageable and match the differential spike's lesson that unexplained ok-pairs need claim cites. CLI-level default covers coarse regressions without a Hypothesis grid for every function.

## Counter-argument

Auto-discovery would find more diffs with no authoring. It also floods the report with noise, which buried the signal in early spike work.
