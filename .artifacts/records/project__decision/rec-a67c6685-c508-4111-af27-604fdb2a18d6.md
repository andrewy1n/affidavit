## Rationale

`aff verdict` is the common technique run. Auto-creating a binding on every recorded verdict would collapse record and earn for the path agents use most, undoing the bind split. Verdict may keep writing unbound observations when a store is present; an agent (or later skill) binds when the observation is meant to earn the claim.

## Counter-argument

Auto-bind on a passing verdict would cut friction and match today’s habit of treating a green verdict as done. That convenience reintroduces “every technique run looks like acceptance” for the dominant technique and makes unbind the rare path instead of the explicit earn step.
