## Rationale

`execute-phase` tells the executor to run `git commit -m "<task-slug>"`, which is why recent subjects are bare slugs with empty bodies. The subject follows Conventional Commits: `type(scope): summary`, with `type` one of `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `refactor`, `test`, or `chore`. The body is one or two sentences on why. A task commit names the task slug in the body so the work-item is still findable.

## Counter-argument

Keeping the slug as the subject makes `git log --oneline` name the work-item directly. A conventional subject drops that link to a line in the body, and a reader scanning subjects has to already know that `fix(verdict)` is the `verdict-command` task.
