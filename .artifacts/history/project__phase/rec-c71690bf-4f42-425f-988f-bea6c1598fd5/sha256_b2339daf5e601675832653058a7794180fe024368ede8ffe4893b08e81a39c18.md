## Problem

Techniques (verdict, audit, probes) produce results, but humans and future agents have no stable, re-checkable trail that ties those results to claims. Without an evidence interface, "done" stays chat or suite-green.

## Approach

Adopt store-backed observations (decisions: `observation-ledger-home`, `aff-observation-seam`, `observation-record-optional`). Claims stay in `.affidavit/manifest.json`. Add `project:evidence-observation` to the contract. `aff verdict` records via the `adaptive-artifacts` CLI when a store is present; `aff trail` lists them. Explicit bind, technique ingest, and orchestra skill policy are later phases (`evidence-bind`, `evidence-technique-ingest`, `evidence-workflow`). Spec/design: `evidence-interface`.

## Exit criteria

- Contract validates with `project:evidence-observation`.
- Fixture: with store, verdict records an observation; `aff trail` shows it; verdict still passes.
- Without store, verdict stdout/exit unchanged.
- Phase tests for the above pass under `python3 -m pytest` for the touched files.
